Why Is It Important?

The Book of Mormon is important for the modern latter-day Saints, and not just them, to all people of the world because this book contains the restored fullness of the gospel of Jesus Christ. Compared to the Old Testament and New Testament which lack the vital details and proper instruction of His gospel, the Book of Mormon is complete and have stated by the prophets who wrote there the plain and true gospel of Jesus Christ. With the help of the Bible and the Book of Mormon working together, all mankind can receive the blessings and promises of salvation and exaltation, meaning eternal life as Gods and Goddesses.
