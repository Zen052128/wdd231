Individual Website Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Another Testament of Jesus Christ

The name was selected because the website is dedicated to introducing people to the Book of Mormon as another testament of Jesus Christ. It immediately communicates the purpose of the website while remaining welcoming to visitors who may know little or nothing about the book.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to teach visitors about the Book of Mormon, its history, and its significance within The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The website will explain where the Book of Mormon came from, how it was translated, why members study it, and how it strengthens faith in Jesus Christ. Visitors will also be able to watch a short video depicting Christ's appearance in the Americas and will be invited to request a free copy through ComeUntoChrist.org.

3. Target Audience

  • Curious visitors
  • Knowledge seekers
  • Pastors
  • Teachers
  • Students of Christianity

4. Visitor Scenarios

  1. What is the Book of Mormon and why should I read it?
  2. Where can I get a free copy of the Book of Mormon? Is it really free?

5. Color Scheme

Color Hex Purpose
#0B132B Header, Navigation, Footer, Buttons
#1C2541 Section Headings
#3A506B Cards, Borders, Highlights
#F5F7FA Page Background

6. Typography

Merriweather

Merriweather will be used throughout the entire website. It offers excellent readability for long passages of text while giving the site a classic and reverent appearance appropriate for religious content.

7. Planned Pages

  • Home — Introduction to the Book of Mormon.
  • History — History of the Book of Mormon, the gold plates, and Joseph Smith.
  • Learn More — Christ's ministry in the Americas, the significance of the Book of Mormon today, and a link to ComeUntoChrist.org.

8. Home Page Features

  • Hero image
  • Introduction section
  • History preview
  • Importance of the Book of Mormon
  • Embedded video
  • Invitation button linking to ComeUntoChrist.org
