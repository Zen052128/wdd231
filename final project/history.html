THE BOOK OF MORMON

A Record Preserved Across Generations

The Book of Mormon is a record of prophets and peoples who lived in the ancient Americas. Their writings tell a story of faith, revelation, conflict, repentance, and the ministry of Jesus Christ.

"And we talk of Christ, we rejoice in Christ, we preach of Christ." 2 Nephi 25:26

THE RESTORATION

From Ancient Record to Modern Scripture

The prophets of the Book of Mormon knew that their record would be preserved for a future generation. Moroni, the final prophet and historian of the record, buried the plates in the earth and preserved them until the time appointed by God.

Centuries later, Moroni appeared as an angel to the young Joseph Smith and instructed him concerning the ancient record. Joseph Smith eventually received the plates and translated the record through the gift and power of God.

The Book of Mormon was first published in 1830 and continues to serve as another witness of Jesus Christ.
