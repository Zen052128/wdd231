UNDERSTANDING THE BOOK

What Is the Book of Mormon?

The Book of Mormon is a record of God's dealings with people who lived in the ancient Americas. It was written by prophets who taught their people about faith, repentance, the commandments of God, and the coming of Jesus Christ.

Like the Bible, the Book of Mormon is a witness of Jesus Christ. Its central message is that Jesus Christ is the Savior of the world and that all people can come unto Him through faith, repentance, baptism, and following His teachings.

WHY IT MATTERS

A Witness of Jesus Christ

The Book of Mormon contains a message intended not only for members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, but for all who are willing to learn about Jesus Christ.

01

Jesus Christ

The Book of Mormon teaches repeatedly about Jesus Christ, His Atonement, His teachings, and His invitation for all people to come unto Him.

02

The Restored Gospel

Latter-day Saints believe the Book of Mormon is a companion witness to the Bible and an important part of the Restoration of the gospel of Jesus Christ.

03

A Personal Witness

Readers are invited to study its message, ponder its teachings, and pray to know for themselves whether its message is true.
